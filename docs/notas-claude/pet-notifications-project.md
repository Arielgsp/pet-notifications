Original request (2026-09-22, session "Mascota notificadora móvil"): phone-only app; a user-chosen pet (dog, cat, duck…) ALWAYS on screen notifies events/reminders; interactions (2 taps → heart, 3 taps → 360° turn); alert = first an onomatopoeia from the mouth ("¡Guau!", "¡Miau!"), then a comic speech bubble; settings for what tapping the bubble does (open, delete, etc.) or auto-hide after X seconds vs. stay until an action; data from in-app reminders or Gmail calendar.

Decisions: Android only (iOS forbids drawing over other apps); Kotlin + Compose overlay service; Gmail via device-synced calendars (CalendarContract, no OAuth); no infinite idle animations (battery) — only occasional breathe/blink; window animations disabled; the FX (bubble) window has a FIXED height — a WRAP_CONTENT window that shrank to 0 left a frozen, translucent, untouchable ghost of the bubble on a real phone (2026-09-25).

Redesign (2026-09-22/23, design session): full-body chibi pets focused on tenderness (canvas "Mascotas tiernas", https://claude.ai/artifact/1qVBbyPqc2YE6LxjDcq3uy). Duck is butter-yellow (user disliked the white one). 3 taps = slow turn front→side→back→mirrored side with ±5° wobble (user rejected the flat 2D spin as not fluid). Sleep: lies down then sleeps after N h without alerts and/or during user bedtime (e.g. 22:00–07:00); new alert or tap wakes it. Birds fluff up instead of lying down. Drawables are generated by `tools/pet_art/export_android.py` — never hand-edit `res/drawable/pet_*.xml` or `PetArt.kt`.

Behavior as of 1.0.10 (2026-10-01): taps 1=hearts, 2=jump, 3=spin; flicks (velocity-based, pet leans then settles) left=upcoming panel, down=quick-note overlay card (presets + inline date/time), up=pause 1h, right=toggle Prefs.muted (alerts silent until next flick). Tap pet closes current bubble/panel. Sleep: 10 min idle (pet touches, FLAG_WATCH_OUTSIDE_TOUCH outside touches, alerts) -> sleeps; wakes on SCREEN_ON/USER_PRESENT, pet touch, quick note. 'Hora de dormir' replaced by 'No molestar' schedule (same bedtime prefs): always asleep, alert wakes it, re-sleeps after 1 min. zzz drawn top-left near head. Watchdog alarm every 15 min restarts pet if Xiaomi kills it; Diagnostics logs unexpected closures. Emulator tip: on this PC use -memory 2048 -gpu swiftshader_indirect (otherwise black screen); adb input can't produce real flicks (too slow).
