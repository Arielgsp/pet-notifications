Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In short: MimuPet has no permission to use the internet. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, and your reminders stay on your phone: we don’t receive or see them.

What’s stored on your phone

This data is stored only on your device, so the app can work:

  • Your reminders and their notes.
  • Your chosen pet, its name, its outfit and its bond level.
  • Your settings.
  • A technical log of alerts from the last 30 days, for the Diagnostics screen.

Permissions and what they’re for

  • Display over other apps: so the pet can appear on your screen. It doesn’t read what’s on it.
  • Notifications: to alert you when the pet isn’t visible.
  • Exact alarms: so alerts arrive on time.
  • Calendar (optional, Pro): to alert you about your events. It only reads the calendars on your phone and never changes them. That data doesn’t leave your device.
  • Device startup and foreground service: so the pet and alerts keep working after a restart.
  • Google Play billing: for in-app purchases.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play with your Google account. We don’t see or receive your payment details. The Google privacy policy applies.

Android backup

If Android backup is on, the system may save the app’s data to your own Google account. Android controls this, and you can turn it off in your phone’s settings. We have no access to that backup.

The pet’s voice

If you turn on the voice, your phone’s speech engine reads only the pet’s sound, like “Woof”. It doesn’t read your reminders.

If you write to us

“Send feedback” opens your email app with a technical summary: app and Android version, phone model, language, plan, permission status, and the number and delay of alerts. It doesn’t include your reminders or events, and you can delete anything before sending it. We only receive that email if you send it, and we use it to reply and improve the app.

How to delete your data

Since it’s on your phone, you can delete it by uninstalling the app or from Settings › Apps › MimuPet › Storage › Clear data. To delete an email you sent us, just ask.

Children

We don’t collect personal data from anyone, including children.

This website

It uses no cookies or tracking. It remembers your language and whether you muted the voice in your browser. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as with any download. Pet voices are generated by your browser, and some browsers use online voices. Links to Google Play and social networks lead to sites with their own policies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with its date.

Contact

Write to the contact email listed on MimuPet’s Google Play page.
